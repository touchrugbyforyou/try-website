Every player deserves a try.

Touch Rugby for You (TRY) funds need-based scholarships for tournament entry, travel, and lodging, so cost isn't what decides who gets to compete.

See how scholarships work

Why TRY exists

In the US, touch rugby runs on players' own money. Every tournament means an entry fee, and the big ones mean flights and hotel rooms on top. For a lot of good players, that adds up to staying home.

TRY raises money from people who care about the sport and turns it into scholarships, awarded on financial need through an open application. Many of our supporters' employers match their gifts, so each donation can go twice as far.

How scholarships work

  1. Apply

    Fill out a short application for an upcoming tournament on our eligible list.

  2. Review

    An independent committee scores applications, with financial need first. Anyone with a personal connection to an applicant sits out.

  3. Award

    We cover all or part of your entry fee, travel, or lodging, paid directly to the tournament or travel provider.

  4. Play

    You compete. Afterward, we may ask you to share a few words about the experience.

Questions

Can I donate so a specific player gets a scholarship?

No. To keep TRY fair and keep gifts tax-deductible, donors can't choose who receives a scholarship. Every scholarship is decided by our independent review committee based on financial need.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

TRY is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation, and our application for federal 501(c)(3) status is pending. We'll post an update here as soon as it's approved.

Will my employer match my gift?

Many employers match donations to 501(c)(3) nonprofits, often dollar for dollar. Check your company's giving portal or ask HR. Once our 501(c)(3) status is confirmed, you can request a match after you give.

How are scholarship recipients chosen?

An independent committee of people who don't play touch rugby scores every application, with financial need weighed first. If a committee member has a personal connection to an applicant for any reason, they sit out that decision.

Can board members or their families apply?

No. To avoid any conflict of interest, TRY board members and their immediate families aren't eligible for scholarships.

How can I help besides donating?

We're looking for volunteers, tournament partners, and people who can spread the word. Email contact@touchrugbyforyou.org.

Who we are

TRY is run by a volunteer board that includes touch rugby players and members from outside the touch community, so scholarship decisions get an independent perspective.

Questions, partnership ideas, or want to volunteer? Email contact@touchrugbyforyou.org or follow us on Instagram at @touchrugbyforyou.

Can you score a try?

Beat the defence with a scoop play. Every try adds more defenders.

  1. Run the ball up. Wherever you’re touched, you dump it and the defence drops back 7 m.
  2. Scoop it with your half, then run or pass. Passes go to your best-placed teammate, never forward.
  3. The half can’t score, and if the half is touched holding the ball, it’s a changeover. Anyone else who’s touched dumps it again, up to six touches.

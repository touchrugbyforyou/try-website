Today we're opening donations for Touch Rugby for You (TRY), a new California nonprofit that helps touch rugby players get to tournaments they couldn't otherwise afford.

## Why we started TRY

Anyone who has played touch knows how welcoming it is. It's fast, social, and easy to pick up, and it brings together people who might never meet otherwise.

But in the US, competing runs on players' own money. Every tournament means an entry fee. The big ones, like Nationals or international events, mean flights and hotel rooms on top. For a lot of good players, that adds up to staying home, and the teams that travel end up shaped by who can pay, not just who can play.

We started TRY to change that. Our belief is simple: every player deserves a try.

## How scholarships will work

- **What we cover:** all or part of a player's tournament entry fee, travel, or lodging.
- **Who decides:** players apply through an open application. An independent committee of people from outside the touch rugby community scores every application, with financial need weighed first. Anyone with a personal connection to an applicant sits out that decision.
- **Who doesn't:** TRY's officers never see applications and have no part in choosing recipients. Board members and their families can't apply.
- **Where the money goes:** we pay tournaments and travel providers directly, not players.

## What happens next

We're raising money first. Once we know how much is in the scholarship fund, we'll choose our first tournament: either the **2027 USA Touch Nationals** or the **2028 Touch World Cup**. We'll announce the application window here and on Instagram at [@touchrugbyforyou](https://www.instagram.com/touchrugbyforyou/).

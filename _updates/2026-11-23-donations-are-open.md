Today we're opening donations for Touch Rugby for You (TRY), a new nonprofit that helps touch rugby players get to tournaments they couldn't otherwise afford.

## Why we started TRY

Anyone who has played touch knows how great it is. It's fast, social, and easy to pick up, and it brings together people who might never meet otherwise.

But in the US, competing runs on players' own money. Every tournament means a cost a player has to pay. The big ones, like USA Touch Nationals or international events, mean flights and hotel rooms on top. For a lot of good players, that adds up to staying home, and the teams that travel end up shaped by who can pay, not just who can play.

We started TRY to change that. Our belief is simple: every player deserves a try.

## How scholarships will work

- **What we cover:** all or part of a player's tournament entry fee, travel, or lodging.
- **Who decides:** players apply through an open application. An independent committee of people from outside the touch rugby community scores every application, with financial need weighed first. No person in the touch community will ever see your application.
- **Where the money goes:** we pay tournaments and travel providers directly, not players.

## What happens next

We're raising money first. Once we know how much is in the scholarship fund, we'll choose our first tournament: either the 2027 USA Touch Nationals or the 2028 Touch World Cup.

## How you can help

- **[Donate.](https://givebutter.com/touch-rugby-for-you)** Every gift goes into the scholarship fund. TRY is a 501(c)(3), so donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
- **Double it.** Many employers match donations to nonprofits, often dollar for dollar. After you give, request a match in your company's giving portal using our EIN, 43‑2815674.
- **[Volunteer.](/#faq)** We need help with our website, social media, and grant research, and many employers donate to nonprofits for the hours you volunteer.
- **Share this post** with a teammate, your club, or anyone who loves the game.
